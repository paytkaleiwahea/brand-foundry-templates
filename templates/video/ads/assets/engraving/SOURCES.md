# Engraving image sources

Every image in this folder is a processed copy of a public-domain or CC0 file from
Wikimedia Commons, downloaded on 2026-10-07. Processing was local only: grayscale,
levels to pure white paper and black ink, crops, the snowflakes inverted, and the
museum photographs cut out with `hyperframes remove-background`.
